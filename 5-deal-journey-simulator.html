Rules used (draft)
  • Signed Up: deal created from Zenoti sync, owner assigned by home studio.
  • First Class Booked: first booking in Zenoti.
  • Attended: first check-in. Engaged: 3+ check-ins.
  • Lapsed (during trial): no check-in by end of Day 4, or 3+ days since last check-in. A new check-in moves the deal back.
  • Trial Expired (after trial): Day 8 with no purchase, whatever the activity. Activity level is kept as a property, not a separate pipeline.
  • Closed Won: any membership or pack purchase, Day 1–14.
  • Closed Lost: Day 15 with no purchase. Reason is set automatically from activity.
  • No-show on a booked class triggers a reschedule email; second no-show creates a seller task.
